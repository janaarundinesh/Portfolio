M.Sc. project Scientific computing 2025

FHP-1 Lattice Gas Simulation

A fluid modelled as particles hopping on a hexagonal lattice — built in serial and MPI, then validated against the kinetic theory it came from.

  • FHP-1 lattice-gas automaton in C on a 300 × 100 hexagonal lattice, with periodic, Dirichlet, no-slip and slip boundaries plus flow around a circular obstacle.
  • MPI decomposition into vertical stripes with one ghost column per side and two Sendrecv exchanges per step; results stay bitwise identical to the serial run on 1–8 ranks.
  • Counter-based hashing of (seed, step, column, row) keeps random numbers independent of the rank count, so any decomposition can be compared directly.
  • Validated shear-wave viscosity against kinetic theory, measured strong scaling, and produced the figures with Python and MATLAB.
